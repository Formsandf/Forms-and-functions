The foundation

Formed inside the maisons.

Nour's career was built in some of retail's most demanding environments. As a store director, and in support of locations across other houses, she learned the business at its highest level.

Her defining chapter was Brunello Cucinelli. There, every piece carries the weight of its craft and its price, and success is earned one client at a time. It is where her standard was set, and where she proved she could meet it.

"In luxury, nothing is left to chance. Not the product, not the service, not the result."

Why Forms and Functions

Craftsmanship deserves to be seen.

The great houses already have their stage. Yet some of the most beautiful pieces, the finest craftsmanship and the most genuine service live elsewhere: in independent boutiques, and with designers at the beginning of their story.

Forms and Functions exists for them. It brings the rigour of a luxury house to brands that are just starting out, so that what they do best is finally seen, felt and sold.
